How SkinHealth AI Works

A step-by-step technical walkthrough of the image analysis pipeline.

1

Image Capture & Validation

User uploads or captures a skin lesion image. The system validates image format (JPG, PNG, WEBP), file size, resolution (min 100x100), and calculates blur variance using OpenCV Laplacian filtering.

2

OpenCV Preprocessing & CLAHE

The image is converted to LAB color space. Contrast Limited Adaptive Histogram Equalization (CLAHE) is applied to the L-channel for boundary clarity, resized to 224x224, and normalized.

3

MobileNetV2 Neural Network

The preprocessed tensor passes through a pretrained MobileNetV2 deep neural network. Bottleneck feature maps pass through GlobalAveragePooling2D, Dense, and Dropout regularization layers.

4

Softmax Probability & Thresholding

The classification head outputs Softmax probabilities across 8 skin disease classes. The engine ranks probabilities descending and checks against the 50% confidence threshold.

5

Guidance & History Sync

Generates tailored skincare advice, ABCDE melanoma checks, and dermatologist consultation steps, while logging predictions to SQLite and optional Supabase cloud storage.
